.isnot

Introducing GLO, section 08. Calm and scannable, not legal fine print.

More capability does not remove your control.

What GLO is

  • Access and capacity that Gia can use under applicable terms.
  • Glonari’s Membership & Access Token.
  • Labeled by status, so you can see what works today.

What GLO is not

  • Not the intelligence, and it does not perform the work by itself.
  • It does not pay income, interest or yield.
  • Transferability does not promise liquidity, resale or appreciation.

Optional line drawing above the title (96px area, 72px on phones, aligned left, gold through currentColor). It draws itself each time the card comes into view, parts 0.1s apart. The second card has an empty slot, which takes no space. With a mouse, a soft gold glow follows the pointer inside each card. Test drawings only; the real marks come with step 5.
